beforeScrollCallback

Targeted by e2e/before-scroll.spec.ts. Every scroll the dropdown does on its own (keyboard, navigate-mode search, reveal on open, scrollTo*()) goes through beforeScrollCallback (rulebook D-CA-5). Each instance records its calls in el.scrollLog; el.scrollAnswer is what the callback returns.
